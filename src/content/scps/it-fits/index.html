Special Containment Procedures

{{SCP_ID}} is contained within Vessel 8 at Site-64. Vessel 8 consists of three concentric welded-steel shells separated by unoccupied inspection galleries. The innermost shell has no drains, vents, conduits, observation ports, or other permanent openings. Its transfer aperture was closed by a continuous full-penetration weld after {{SCP_ID}} was placed inside.

All three shells are monitored by ultrasonic crack detectors, laser interferometers, and load cells. Any newly detected crack, deformation, or sustained shift in load distribution requires immediate evacuation of the containment sector, regardless of whether the defect appears to penetrate the affected shell. Inspection of the galleries and repair of Vessel 8 are to be performed remotely.

No routinely occupied area is permitted within 40 m of the exterior surface of Vessel 8. Personnel who hear scraping from within a wall, floor, pipe, or structural member within this radius are to evacuate the containment sector without investigating.

{{SCP_ID}} has shown no measurable decline during sealed confinement. Food, water, and fresh air are not to be supplied.

Description

{{SCP_ID}} is a hairless, grey-white organism measuring approximately 4.8 m in length and weighing 312 kg. It normally supports itself on six limbs. Its anterior end resembles an elongated human head lacking visible eyes, ears, and nostrils. A mouth extends across the underside of the head and contains several rows of narrow teeth.

{{SCP_ID}} can narrow and elongate its body sufficiently to enter openings far smaller than any normal cross-section of the organism. Bone, nerves, blood vessels, and other structures become correspondingly thin but remain continuous. Its total mass does not measurably change.

Movement while compressed is slow and may proceed at only a few millimeters per hour. Portions of the organism that have already emerged from an opening are capable of rapid movement. The narrowest aperture through which {{SCP_ID}} has been confirmed to extend tissue measured 1.3 mm at its widest point. No lower limit has been established.

The internal dimensions of {{SCP_ID}} do not consistently correspond to its exterior. During one examination, an endoscope was advanced 9.1 m into the upper digestive tract without reaching an obstruction, although {{SCP_ID}} measured less than 5 m at the time. The instrument subsequently entered a cavity approximately 1.7 m across while physically located within a section of the organism measuring 23 cm in diameter. No corresponding cavity was visible during a CT scan conducted twenty-three minutes later.

{{SCP_ID}} is capable of killing and consuming humans and other animals.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-1: Recovery

{{SCP_ID}} was recovered on 06/14/2009 following the disappearance of the █████ family from their residence in ███████, Colorado.

Daniel █████, Rebecca █████, and their children Thomas, 11, and Erin, 7, were last confirmed inside the house at 21:04. The residence’s security system recorded no exterior door or window opening before a neighbor entered the property the following morning.

Blood was present in the upstairs hallway and utility room. A section of flexible duct connected to the second-floor air return had been pulled from its mounting collar. Human tissue was recovered from inside the duct.

Body-camera footage from the responding officers shows a pale mass occupying most of the duct approximately 1.4 m behind an upstairs return grille. When Officer ███████ removed the grille, the mass traversed the 1.4 m distance to the opening and wrapped around his right forearm. He was pulled against the wall before two other officers were able to free him. The resulting injuries included a fractured clavicle and extensive damage to the hand and wrist.

Foundation personnel located {{SCP_ID}} nine hours later within a stormwater culvert 2.4 km from the residence. {{SCP_ID}} was entering a drainage opening measuring 14 cm by 19 cm. Sedation did not interrupt this movement. By the time {{SCP_ID}} became unresponsive eleven minutes later, more than half of its body had entered the opening.

Recovery required removal of the surrounding reinforced concrete.

Human remains recovered from the digestive tract of {{SCP_ID}} were identified as belonging to Daniel and Rebecca █████. No biological material attributable to either child was identified.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-2: Initial Containment Failure

{{SCP_ID}} was initially housed in Chamber 8-A, a welded enclosure constructed from 32 mm steel plate. The chamber contained no open penetrations after installation.

On 08/31/2009, ultrasonic inspection identified a shallow crack along a lower weld seam. The defect measured less than 0.5 mm at its widest point, and imaging indicated that it did not extend through the chamber wall. Repair preparations began immediately, but evacuation was not considered necessary.

At 02:12 the following morning, Technician Martin Rook reported scraping within a service-corridor wall 12 m from Chamber 8-A. At the time, noise originating outside the chamber was not recognized as evidence of a containment failure. Rook removed an access panel to inspect what he believed was a loose conduit.

A strand of pale tissue emerged from the wall cavity and entered Rook’s left auditory canal. Personnel present were unable to remove it before it penetrated the inner ear and brainstem. Rook died at the scene.

Remote inspection found approximately 37 m of {{SCP_ID}} tissue distributed through wall cavities and unused structural recesses. The extension remained physically connected to the portion of {{SCP_ID}} inside Chamber 8-A and passed through the previously documented weld defect, which had widened into an opening measuring 1.3 mm.

Following evacuation of the containment sector, {{SCP_ID}} withdrew the external tissue over the next seven hours. At no point did the main body of the organism leave Chamber 8-A.

Metallurgical examination found parallel scoring and polished surfaces along the interior of the breached seam. Review of load-cell records showed that {{SCP_ID}} had kept most of its body mass pressed against the affected section of wall for approximately eleven weeks. This behavior had previously been interpreted as inactivity.

After {{SCP_ID}} had fully withdrawn, the breached seam was sealed remotely. Chamber 8-A was enclosed within two temporary welded-steel shells, and the surrounding sector remained evacuated while Vessel 8 was constructed.

{{SCP_ID}} was transferred to Vessel 8 on 11/18/2009. Chamber 8-A was decommissioned following confirmation that all three shells of Vessel 8 had been successfully sealed.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-3: Supplemental Search of Recovery Site

Following the Chamber 8-A incident, the █████ residence was searched again under the assumption that {{SCP_ID}} could access spaces connected to the building by defects too small to have been considered during the original investigation.

A structural scan identified a cavity within the reinforced-concrete retaining wall supporting the eastern side of the property. The cavity measured 0.7 m by 0.9 m by 1.4 m. Its only connection to the surrounding structure was an irregular shrinkage fissure measuring 2.4 mm at its widest point.

The body of Erin █████ was recovered from inside.

The concrete surrounding the cavity dated to the original construction of the residence. No removable panel, construction joint, pipe, or former access point was present.

Erin had not been consumed or otherwise mutilated. She had died from dehydration an estimated four to six days after the disappearance of her family. Concrete dust was present in her airways and beneath her fingernails.

Her remains were fully articulated. No fractures, dislocations, or injuries consistent with compression were found.

Thomas █████ has not been located.
