Special Containment Procedures

{{SCP_ID}} is contained in a reinforced concrete chamber at Site-64. The chamber is equipped with two separately powered arrays of recessed industrial horn loudspeakers configured to produce continuous broadband noise. Either array must be capable of independently preventing reliable directed movement by {{SCP_ID}}. All emitters are mounted behind perforated steel guards anchored directly to the chamber structure. No wiring, conduit, or service access is exposed within the chamber.

As a non-electrical emergency system, six water motor gongs supplied by the Site-64 fire-water main are mounted in protected wall recesses. These devices are intended only to impair {{SCP_ID}} during simultaneous failure of both powered arrays. Their activation does not restore normal containment status.

Entry into the chamber is prohibited unless both powered arrays are operational. Authorized personnel are to wear hearing protection with integrated radio communication and carry a battery-powered personal alarm capable of producing sustained high-volume noise.

During a containment breach, personnel are not to hide, remain motionless, or attempt silent movement. Personal alarms are to be activated immediately. Personnel without a functioning unit are to shout continuously and strike nearby walls, pipes, doors, or equipment while evacuating.

Personnel are not to stop producing noise in order to determine {{SCP_ID}}’s location. {{SCP_ID}} will attack exposed acoustic devices; fixed alarms and loudspeakers are not to be treated as shelter or rally points.

Description

{{SCP_ID}} is a predatory quadrupedal organism approximately 2.1 m long and weighing approximately 190 kg. Its body is hairless and dark gray. The forelimbs are substantially more developed than the hind limbs and terminate in five elongated digits capable of gripping, pulling, and prying. {{SCP_ID}} possesses no functional eyes.

The organism’s feet are covered by broad, deformable pads. Despite its mass, {{SCP_ID}} produces exceptionally little audible noise while moving. Recordings made across concrete, tile, wood, and compacted soil frequently contain no sound distinguishable from background noise.

{{SCP_ID}} navigates through an acoustic process consistent with echolocation. Periodic low-amplitude emissions associated with the organism have been recorded, but attempts to isolate a stable ranging signal have been unsuccessful. Whether these emissions account for the full extent of {{SCP_ID}}’s perception is unknown.

In low-noise conditions, {{SCP_ID}} can navigate complex interior spaces without hesitation and locate stationary human-sized targets through common interior partitions. Sustained broadband noise interferes with this ability. Ordinary conversation, footsteps, and breathing have little measurable effect.

The impairment is gradual. Under heavy acoustic interference, {{SCP_ID}} moves slowly, collides with obstacles, probes nearby surfaces, and frequently abandons direct pursuit. As ambient noise decreases, its movements become faster and more deliberate. In sufficiently quiet environments, {{SCP_ID}} has reached approximately 48 km/h over short distances while navigating multiple turns without collision.

{{SCP_ID}} does not avoid loud environments or display physiological signs of distress when exposed to them. When subjected to intense concentrated sound, it will strike, tear, or sweep through the general area of the dominant source until the sound ceases. These attacks are poorly directed and frequently damage surrounding walls, fixtures, and unrelated objects.

Whenever possible, {{SCP_ID}} selects routes that produce the least noise. It will bypass doors, partitions, and other obstacles when a quieter route is available, even when doing so substantially increases the distance to its target. If no such route exists, {{SCP_ID}} will manipulate locks and handles, attack structural weak points, or breach the obstruction by force.

The organism can penetrate ordinary interior walls, break wooden doors, deform light sheet-metal barriers, damage ventilation systems, and force open many conventional security doors. It cannot rapidly breach reinforced concrete, heavy structural steel, or purpose-built containment doors. {{SCP_ID}} frequently pauses following loud impacts before resuming destructive activity.

{{SCP_ID}} has never been observed retreating from sound.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}.1 — Recovery

{{SCP_ID}} came to Foundation attention following the deaths of five employees at a partially decommissioned industrial processing facility outside Redding, California. The facility had ceased normal production approximately eight months earlier but remained partially powered while machinery was being removed and sold.

On 2023-11-08, a six-person maintenance team entered the property to inspect electrical systems and equipment scheduled for dismantling. At approximately 09:40, the team restored power to several sections of the facility. Multiple ventilation fans, conveyor motors, and two industrial shredding units began operating.

No unusual activity was reported during the following three hours.

At 12:52, team supervisor Kevin Marr ordered the machinery shut down so the group could inspect a damaged electrical cabinet. Marcus Sutter later reported hearing “something heavy” in the loading area approximately twenty seconds after the machinery stopped.

Two employees separated from the group to investigate. Neither returned.

At 13:07, the four remaining employees entered an administrative office and contacted emergency services. The following call was recovered from local dispatch records.

Dispatcher: 911. What’s the address of your emergency?

Marr: Old processing plant off Clear Creek Road. Something’s in here.

Dispatcher: What do you mean by something?

Marr: I don’t know. Two guys are missing. We heard something in the loading area.

Dispatcher: Are you in a safe location?

Marr: We’re in an office. Door’s locked.

Dispatcher: Stay where you are. Deputies are on the way.

Marr: It’s quiet now.

Dispatcher: Can you hear anyone outside the room?

Marr: No.

Dispatcher: Stay on the line with me.

[Fourteen-second pause.]

Marr: There’s something at the door.

Dispatcher: What do you see?

Marr: Nothing. It’s—

[Impact.]

Marr: Jesus Christ.

[Second impact.]

Marr: It’s coming through.

Dispatcher: Get away from the door.

Marr: Where do we go?

[Metal deformation audible.]

Marr: Oh fuck—

[Call terminates.]

Responding deputies located Sutter approximately 400 m from the facility. After observing movement through a loading-bay window, they withdrew pending tactical support. Foundation personnel embedded in the subsequent state response assumed control of the property.

Five bodies were recovered following the capture of {{SCP_ID}}. Sutter had sustained a fractured wrist, multiple lacerations, and partial hearing loss.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}.2 — Acoustic Testing

Field personnel initially hypothesized that {{SCP_ID}} possessed unusually sensitive hearing and avoided loud environments. Controlled testing did not support this interpretation. {{SCP_ID}} displayed no physiological distress when exposed to high-volume noise and made no consistent attempt to move away from active acoustic sources.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-05

A suspended meat target was placed inside a reinforced chamber with {{SCP_ID}}.

Under low-noise conditions, {{SCP_ID}} crossed the chamber and reached the target in 3.2 seconds.

During high-output broadband noise, {{SCP_ID}} searched the chamber for eleven minutes without locating the target. The target remained stationary throughout.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-08

A heated human-sized mannequin was positioned behind a standard interior wall.

Under low-noise conditions, {{SCP_ID}} immediately approached the wall and began breaching it. When broadband noise was introduced, {{SCP_ID}} stopped and moved away.

The acoustic system was then disabled.

{{SCP_ID}} stopped moving. After approximately two seconds, it turned directly toward the mannequin and resumed breaching the wall.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-12

A high-output alarm speaker was activated inside the test chamber.

{{SCP_ID}} began moving irregularly, then struck the wall surrounding the alarm. Its first two strikes missed the speaker. The third destroyed its mounting bracket.

The speaker remained active while suspended from its wiring. {{SCP_ID}} continued striking the wall and adjacent fixtures until the unit was destroyed.

Following cessation of the alarm, {{SCP_ID}} remained motionless for approximately two seconds before resuming normal navigation.

A repeat test using three separately mounted alarms produced attacks throughout the loudest portion of the chamber. {{SCP_ID}} did not consistently identify the location of any individual unit.

Testing with exposed acoustic devices was subsequently discontinued.

Dr. Hannah Pierce appended the following note:

We keep describing the low-noise state as aggression.

It is not.

The animal becomes less agitated when the noise stops.

It stops searching.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}.3 — Incident {{SCP_NUMBER}}-03

On 2024-04-01, a fault in a distribution cabinet then shared by both powered loudspeaker arrays disabled both systems serving {{SCP_ID}} containment.

The water motor gongs activated as designed. They slowed {{SCP_ID}} but did not prevent directed movement.

At 02:14:42, {{SCP_ID}} reached the northern loudspeaker recess and struck the protective steel guard repeatedly. The guard remained intact.

After approximately twenty seconds, {{SCP_ID}} abandoned the attempt and approached an obsolete service chase at the base of the northern wall.

{{SCP_ID}} began breaching the chase. Each impact was followed by a brief pause.

At 02:17:26, {{SCP_ID}} entered Utility Corridor 4.

Emergency acoustic alarms activated throughout the containment sector. {{SCP_ID}} collided with the western wall, then began striking nearby fixtures.

The first exposed alarm was destroyed at 02:17:39. A second was torn from its mounting twelve seconds later. A third ceased functioning at 02:18:11.

With each failure, {{SCP_ID}}’s movements became more controlled.

Security personnel were ordered to evacuate while maintaining continuous noise. One officer threw an activated personal alarm into an intersecting corridor. {{SCP_ID}} redirected toward the device and struck the surrounding floor and walls until it was destroyed. The officer escaped.

Technician Evan Reeve did not comply with the evacuation order. Reeve entered Supply Room 4B, closed the door, silenced his radio, and concealed himself behind shelving.

{{SCP_ID}} was approximately 24 m away.

At 02:19:07, {{SCP_ID}} entered the maintenance corridor adjoining Supply Room 4B.

Reeve remained motionless.

No audible movement from {{SCP_ID}} was recorded.

At 02:19:34, {{SCP_ID}} stopped directly behind the rear wall of Supply Room 4B. The wall consisted of gypsum board over light steel framing.

{{SCP_ID}} began removing material from the wall. It paused following each impact.

At 02:19:52, the impacts stopped.

Reeve waited an additional seventeen seconds.

He then left his hiding place and opened the supply-room door.

{{SCP_ID}} immediately reversed direction.

Reeve attempted to close the door. {{SCP_ID}} reached the room before it could be secured.

Reeve was killed.

One powered loudspeaker array was restored at 02:22:06. {{SCP_ID}} was recontained without further casualties.

The shared distribution cabinet and obsolete service chase were removed during reconstruction of the containment chamber. Both powered arrays are now supplied through fully independent power and control paths.

Following Incident {{SCP_NUMBER}}-03, the following instruction was added to all Site-64 containment drills:

Do not hide.

Do not listen for it.

Make noise and move.
