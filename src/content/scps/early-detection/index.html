Special Containment Procedures

{{SCP_ID}} is installed in Examination Room 4 at Site-22 and may be operated only by Foundation medical personnel. Use requires written authorization from the Site-22 Medical Director and is restricted to cases in which early diagnosis would materially alter treatment.

Repeat examinations are not authorized.

Findings not confirmed through conventional testing are to be recorded as unresolved. Follow-up testing is to be conducted at intervals appropriate to the identified condition and no less than annually. Invasive or prophylactic treatment based solely on an unresolved finding requires Ethics Committee approval.

Description

{{SCP_ID}} is a GE LOGIQ 7 ultrasound system recovered from the ███████ Family Clinic in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, following the retirement of its owner in 2011. Foundation attention was drawn to the device after a review of the clinic’s archived records identified twenty-three patients whose files contained precise diagnoses months or years before supporting clinical evidence became available.

During an examination, {{SCP_ID}} will occasionally display a diagnostic annotation not produced by any software installed on the device. Each annotation identifies a medical condition and its location within the subject. It does not provide treatment recommendations, prognosis, or an estimated date of onset.

{{SCP_ID}} has identified malignant tumors, aneurysms, autoimmune disorders, degenerative neurological conditions, and several anomalous pathologies. Some findings can be confirmed immediately through conventional testing. In other cases, no corresponding pathology is detectable.

Since recovery, {{SCP_ID}} has produced 1,846 anomalous findings. Of these, 1,803 were independently confirmed within thirty days. Early treatment following these findings prevented or substantially reduced permanent injury in 214 cases.

The remaining forty-three findings were initially classified as unresolved.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-1: Validation Trial

In 2014, sixteen subjects who received anomalous annotations during Foundation medical screening were examined a second time within twenty-four hours.

{{SCP_ID}} did not reproduce the initial annotation in any case. Each second examination produced a different diagnosis affecting a different anatomical location.

All sixteen initial findings were confirmed through conventional testing within thirty days. No corresponding pathology associated with any of the second findings was detectable at the time of the trial.

No further repeat examinations were authorized.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-2: Longitudinal Review

A longitudinal review of the forty-three findings not confirmed within thirty days was completed in 2025.

Thirty-nine have since been confirmed. In every case, the diagnosis, affected organ, and location matched the original annotation. The interval between examination and conventional confirmation ranged from four months to eleven years and four months.

This total includes fourteen of the sixteen second findings produced during the 2014 validation trial.

Four findings remain unresolved. Two were produced outside the validation trial and have remained unresolved for four years and two months and eight years and six months, respectively.

The other two were produced during the validation trial. They have remained unresolved for eleven years and one month and eleven years and three months.

No finding produced by {{SCP_ID}} has remained unresolved for longer than eleven years and four months.
