Special Containment Procedures

{{SCP_ID}} is contained in Chamber 14 of Sublevel 3, Site-██. The chamber is to remain unlit.

The entrance to Chamber 14 consists of three steel doors separated by 2 m corridors. Doors 14-A and 14-B are mechanically operated and require separate keys. Door 14-C has been welded shut.

The keys to 14-A and 14-B are to be held by separate security officers who have not previously entered Chamber 14. Neither officer is permitted below Sublevel 2 except during an authorized containment operation.

No photographic, video, or optical recording equipment is permitted within Sublevel 3.

Personnel are not to look through the observation port in Door 14-B.

The observation port is to remain covered.

Following Incident {{SCP_NUMBER}}-4, personnel assigned to {{SCP_ID}} are prohibited from asking why the observation port has not been removed.

Requests to modify these procedures require approval from the Site Director and two Level-4 personnel from another Foundation facility.

Under no circumstances is {{SCP_ID}} to be shown to personnel for testing purposes.

Description

{{SCP_ID}} is located in the center of Chamber 14.

Its physical characteristics are unknown.

Personnel who directly observe {{SCP_ID}} become convinced that opening Door 14-C is necessary.

The stated reason varies between subjects.

No anomalous effect has been observed when {{SCP_ID}} is concealed behind an opaque barrier. {{SCP_ID}} has never moved from its present location and has demonstrated no ability to affect physical objects.

The interior surface of Door 14-C contains numerous scratches. Their origin is unknown.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-1: Recovery

{{SCP_ID}} was recovered on 03/11/2008 following the disappearance of five employees at the ███████ Municipal Water Treatment Facility in ███████, Pennsylvania.

Local police discovered the facility unlocked and unoccupied.

Security footage showed night supervisor Thomas Vale entering Pump Room 6 at 23:41.

At 23:48, Vale exited the room and telephoned another employee, identified as Marcus Bell.

Bell arrived at 00:17.

The two men entered Pump Room 6 together.

At 00:22, Bell telephoned a third employee.

This continued until all five night-shift employees were present.

At 01:09, Vale called emergency services.

The following is a partial transcript.

DISPATCH: 911. What's the address of your emergency?

VALE: ███████ Water. South intake building.

DISPATCH: What's happening there?

VALE: We found somebody.

DISPATCH: Is the person injured?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Are they conscious?

VALE: Yes.

DISPATCH: Can I speak to them?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Why not?

[11 seconds silence.]

VALE: There's a door in the way.

DISPATCH: Is the person trapped?

VALE: I think so.

DISPATCH: Can you open the door?

VALE: We're trying.

DISPATCH: Is there a fire or another immediate danger?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Then I need you to stop trying to force the door until responders arrive.

[Unidentified voice in background.]

VALE: We can't do that.

DISPATCH: Why?

[18 seconds silence.]

VALE: He's been in there a long time.

The call continued for approximately four minutes.

Police arrived at 01:21.

Pump Room 6 was empty.

A section of concrete wall had been removed using maintenance equipment. Behind it was a narrow service passage not present on facility plans.

The passage terminated approximately 11 m underground at a steel door.

Four of the five employees were found immediately outside the door.

All had suffered injuries to the hands and forearms consistent with prolonged attempts to force it open.

Vale was not present.

The door was open approximately 4 cm.

Officer █████ █████ looked through the opening.

He subsequently shot Officer ███████ in the abdomen when the latter attempted to close the door.

Foundation personnel embedded in the ███████ County Sheriff's Department were notified at 02:03.

The door was sealed at 05:46.

Vale has not been recovered.

[Subject does not respond.]

Dr. Morland: D-8821?

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-02

Subject: D-1904

D-1904 was not informed of Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01.

Following seven seconds of exposure, D-1904 attempted to open Door 14-C.

Subject was removed from the chamber.

When questioned, D-1904 stated that {{SCP_ID}} was a young girl.

Subject claimed the girl was trapped in Chamber 14 and that Door 14-C led out of the chamber.

When informed that Door 14-C led further underground, D-1904 became agitated and accused research staff of lying.

Architectural plans were shown to the subject.

D-1904 examined the plans for approximately six minutes.

Subject then identified a ventilation shaft as an alternative route into the space behind Door 14-C.

Testing terminated.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-03

Subject: D-5520

D-5520 was informed before exposure that {{SCP_ID}} would attempt to make him open Door 14-C.

Subject was shown photographs of the exterior of Door 14-C and informed that previous subjects had attempted to open it.

D-5520 was instructed that, regardless of what he saw, he was not to approach the door.

Subject entered Chamber 14 at 13:03.

At 13:04, subject requested permission to leave.

Permission granted.

Subject exited without incident.

At 15:11, D-5520 asked a security officer whether maintenance personnel had inspected the hinges on Door 14-C.

At 19:32, D-5520 complained of chest pain and was transported to Site medical.

At 19:46, subject assaulted attending staff and attempted to access an emergency stairwell leading toward Sublevel 3.

Subject was restrained.

A folded sheet of paper was discovered in D-5520's shoe.

It contained a hand-drawn map of Sublevel 3.

The map included a maintenance corridor D-5520 had never entered.

The corridor terminates behind Door 14-C.

Testing suspended.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-3

During review of recovery documentation, Research Assistant H. Patel noted a discrepancy concerning the original underground chamber.

The steel door discovered beneath the water treatment facility opened toward the service passage.

Damage documented at the scene indicated that Vale and the other employees had been attempting to pull the door open.

The door had no exterior handle.

It did possess an interior handle.

No evidence was found that this handle had ever been used.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-4: Incident

On 07/19/2011, Senior Researcher Morland requested permission to inspect {{SCP_ID}}'s containment chamber.

The request was denied.

At 03:12 the following morning, Morland entered Sublevel 3 using credentials belonging to Dr. ███████.

Security personnel intercepted him outside Door 14-A.

Morland surrendered without resistance.

A hacksaw, bolt cutters, hydraulic spreader, and two flashlights were recovered from a maintenance cart in his possession.

Morland had not directly observed {{SCP_ID}} since Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01, three years earlier.

During interrogation, Morland denied intending to breach containment.

He stated that he wished to inspect Door 14-C.

When asked why, Morland initially refused to answer.

The following exchange occurred approximately two hours into the interview.

Agent Keller: You've spent three years studying this thing. You wrote half the containment procedures yourself.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: You know what happens to people who see it.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: And you still went down there.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: Why?

[Silence.]

Keller: What's behind the door?

Morland: We don't know.

Keller: What do you think is behind it?

Morland: Nothing.

Keller: Nothing?

Morland: The geological survey says solid limestone for another thirty-one meters.

Keller: Then what were you going to do with the spreader?

[Silence.]

Morland: Check.

Keller: Check what?

Morland: That it was still locked.

Keller: You can check that from this side.

Morland: I know.

Keller: Then what did you need the tools for?

[Morland begins crying.]

Morland: I don't know.

Interview terminated.

Morland was removed from {{SCP_ID}} research.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-5

On 08/02/2011, demolition of the observation port in Door 14-B was authorized.

Work was scheduled for 08/05.

On 08/04, Site Director ██████ cancelled the order.

The reason entered into the maintenance system was:

Observation capability may be required during future containment failure.

The cancellation was discovered during a 2014 procedural review.

Director ██████ had never been assigned to {{SCP_ID}} and had no documented exposure.

When questioned, he stated that he did not remember cancelling the work order.

Records confirm the cancellation was entered from his office terminal using his credentials.

The observation port remains in place.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-6

On 11/██/2016, routine ultrasonic inspection of Door 14-C detected a cavity within the limestone behind the chamber.

Previous geological surveys had detected no such cavity.

Ground-penetrating radar was authorized.

Results indicated an enclosed space beginning approximately 0.8 m behind Door 14-C and extending beyond the effective range of the equipment.

Estimated minimum dimensions were 9 m × 14 m × ██ m.

No excavation was authorized.

A second survey conducted from an adjacent service tunnel produced different dimensions.

A third survey was not performed.

At the request of Site Director ██████, all copies of the survey results were sealed under Level-4 classification.

The following handwritten note was found attached to the original report.

Don't measure it again.

First survey says there's a room.

Second says there's a hallway.

Look at the recovery photographs.

There wasn't supposed to be anything behind that door either.

— H.P.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-7

On 04/██/2019, {{SCP_ID}}'s chamber was inspected following a minor seismic event.

No personnel entered Chamber 14.

A fiber-optic camera was inserted beneath Door 14-A to inspect structural damage in the outer corridor.

At 14:22, the camera was advanced approximately 6 cm farther than authorized.

Technician ███████ viewed the monitor.

The feed did not include {{SCP_ID}} or Chamber 14.

Technician ███████ immediately asked whether Door 14-C was open.

The supervising researcher stated that it was not.

███████ replied:

“Then why is there light under it?”

No light was visible on the recording.

███████ attempted to advance the camera farther into the corridor and was restrained.

The camera was withdrawn.

Frame-by-frame review found no visual abnormalities.

Three members of the review team subsequently requested access to the uncompressed recording.

All requests were denied.

The recording was destroyed.
