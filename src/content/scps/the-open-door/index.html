Special Containment Procedures

{{SCP_ID}} is contained within Provisional Site-{{SCP_NUMBER}}, established around the former ███████ Municipal Water Treatment Facility in ███████, Pennsylvania, and the subterranean structure beneath it.

The original subterranean chamber containing {{SCP_ID}} is designated Chamber 14 and is to remain unlit. Access is controlled by two Foundation-installed steel doors, designated 14-A and 14-B, separated by a 2 m corridor. Both doors require separate mechanical keys. The keys are to be held by separate security officers with no history of exposure to {{SCP_ID}}.

Door 14-C is the original steel door located in the northern wall of Chamber 14. It has been welded continuously around its frame and covered by an independent opaque steel barrier anchored to the chamber floor and ceiling.

Human entry into Chamber 14 is prohibited.

Inspection of Door 14-C and the surrounding rock is to be performed remotely using ultrasonic and structural monitoring equipment. No procedure may create a direct human line of sight through Door 14-C or any defect in the surrounding structure.

Personnel previously exposed to {{SCP_ID}} are prohibited from assignment to its containment, maintenance, or administrative review. Recommendations concerning Door 14-C made by exposed personnel are not to be acted upon unless independently reproduced by personnel with no exposure history.

Any proposal to cut, drill, remove, open, or otherwise alter Door 14-C requires approval from the Provisional Site Director and two Level-4 personnel assigned to a separate Foundation facility.

Direct observational testing of {{SCP_ID}} is prohibited.

Description

{{SCP_ID}} designates Door 14-C, the space beyond it, and an associated perceptual effect.

Door 14-C is a steel industrial door of unknown manufacture installed within a roughly excavated limestone wall. The door opens toward Chamber 14. No handle, latch control, or other opening mechanism is present on the chamber-facing side.

A conventional lever handle is mounted on the opposite side.

When Door 14-C is opened sufficiently to permit a human observer to see any portion of the space beyond it, the observer develops a persistent conviction that the door should be opened farther. The intensity of this conviction varies. Most exposed individuals attempt to open Door 14-C immediately or request that others do so.

Most affected individuals report seeing a human figure beyond the threshold. Descriptions are inconsistent and have differed in apparent age, sex, clothing, and physical appearance.

No figure described by an exposed subject has been confirmed in contemporaneous photographic or video recordings.

The reason an affected individual gives for opening Door 14-C varies. Subjects have stated that a person beyond the door requires assistance, that the door has been closed accidentally, that the door is damaged or incorrectly installed, that the space beyond requires inspection, or that opening the door is necessary to verify that it remains secure.

Affected individuals otherwise retain normal memory, reasoning ability, and awareness of {{SCP_ID}}'s documented effects. Informing a subject that the desire to open Door 14-C is anomalous does not reliably alter that desire.

Removal from the vicinity of Chamber 14 substantially reduces immediate attempts to access Door 14-C. The underlying conviction may persist for years and may intensify when exposed individuals are presented with information concerning the door, its condition, or the space beyond it.

No evidence indicates that written descriptions, audio recordings, photographs, or video recordings transmit the effect to individuals without prior direct exposure.

No new exposure has occurred while Door 14-C has remained completely opaque.

No Foundation personnel have entered the space beyond Door 14-C. Geological and structural surveys have produced mutually incompatible measurements of this space. See Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-5.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-1: Recovery

{{SCP_ID}} was discovered on 03/11/2008 following the disappearance of five employees at the ███████ Municipal Water Treatment Facility in ███████, Pennsylvania.

Security footage showed night supervisor Thomas Vale entering Pump Room 6 at 23:41. At 23:48, Vale exited the room and telephoned another employee, Marcus Ward.

Ward arrived at 00:17. The two men entered Pump Room 6 together.

At 00:22, Ward telephoned a third employee. This continued until all five night-shift employees were present.

At 01:09, Vale contacted emergency services.

The following is a partial transcript.

DISPATCH: 911. What's the address of your emergency?

VALE: ███████ Water. South intake building.

DISPATCH: What's happening there?

VALE: We found somebody.

DISPATCH: Is the person injured?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Are they conscious?

VALE: Yes.

DISPATCH: Can I speak to them?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Why not?

[11 seconds silence.]

VALE: There's a door in the way.

DISPATCH: Is the person trapped?

VALE: I think so.

DISPATCH: Can you open the door?

VALE: We're trying.

DISPATCH: Is there a fire or another immediate danger?

VALE: No.

DISPATCH: Then I need you to stop trying to force the door until responders arrive.

[Unidentified voice in background.]

VALE: We can't do that.

DISPATCH: Why?

[18 seconds silence.]

VALE: He's been in there a long time.

The call continued for approximately four minutes.

Police arrived at 01:21.

A section of concrete wall in Pump Room 6 had been removed using maintenance equipment. Behind it was a narrow descending service passage not present on facility plans. The passage terminated approximately 11 m underground in a roughly excavated chamber measuring 7.8 m by 5.1 m. This chamber is now designated Chamber 14.

Door 14-C was located in its northern wall.

Four of the five employees were found immediately in front of Door 14-C. All were conscious but severely exhausted. Two resisted removal from the chamber and repeatedly insisted that Thomas Vale was behind the door.

All four had suffered injuries to the hands and forearms consistent with prolonged attempts to pull Door 14-C open. A hydraulic flange spreader, two pry bars, and a length of chain had been attached to its chamber-facing side.

Vale was not present.

Door 14-C was open approximately 4 cm.

Officer █████ █████ approached the opening after the employees continued to state that Vale was visible beyond it.

█████ looked through the gap.

Body-camera audio records the following statement:

He's right there.

No individual is visible through the opening in the corresponding video.

Officer ███████ moved to push Door 14-C closed.

█████ drew his service weapon and shot ███████ in the abdomen.

He was restrained by two additional officers. During restraint, █████ repeatedly stated that closing the door would "leave him in there."

When asked who he meant, █████ answered:

The man they called about.

█████ had not heard Vale's emergency call and had not spoken with any of the facility employees before viewing the opening.

Foundation personnel embedded in the ███████ County Sheriff's Department were notified at 02:03.

Door 14-C was secured at 05:46.

Thomas Vale was not located.

The Foundation subsequently acquired the water-treatment facility and surrounding property through a holding company. Provisional Site-{{SCP_NUMBER}} was established around the existing subterranean structure.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-2: Initial Testing

The following tests were conducted before the adoption of the current prohibition on direct observation. A temporary hydraulic apparatus attached to the chamber-facing side of Door 14-C allowed the door to be opened and closed remotely.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01

Subject: D-8821

Door 14-C was opened to a width of 3 cm. D-8821 was instructed to observe the opening for five seconds and withdraw.

After approximately two seconds, D-8821 stated:

There's a guy back there.

The subject remained in position for 47 seconds despite repeated instructions to withdraw.

Dr. Morland entered Chamber 14 to remove D-8821 and crossed the observation line for approximately six seconds.

D-8821 subsequently requested a crowbar.

Request denied.

Interview excerpt:

Dr. Morland: What did you see?

D-8821: A guy.

Dr. Morland: Describe him.

D-8821: I don't know. Just a guy.

Dr. Morland: Was he restrained?

D-8821: No.

Dr. Morland: Injured?

D-8821: No.

Dr. Morland: Did he speak to you?

D-8821: No.

Dr. Morland: Then why did you ask for a crowbar?

D-8821: The door.

Dr. Morland: What about it?

D-8821: It's stuck.

Dr. Morland: Why do you want to open it?

[Subject does not respond.]

Dr. Morland: D-8821?

D-8821: You didn't see him.

The test was recorded by two cameras positioned behind D-8821. No individual is visible beyond Door 14-C in either recording.

Nine personnel with no history of exposure reviewed the footage. No anomalous behavior was observed.

Morland reported seeing only "a dark passage" and denied experiencing any unusual reaction. During the post-test review, he opposed resealing Door 14-C until the remaining approved trials had been completed.

This recommendation was consistent with the existing test protocol and was not considered anomalous at the time.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-02

Subject: D-1904

D-1904 was not informed of Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01.

Door 14-C was opened to the same width.

Following seven seconds of exposure, D-1904 attempted to approach the door and was removed from Chamber 14.

When questioned, D-1904 stated that a young girl was standing beyond Door 14-C. No child is visible in the test recording.

D-1904 claimed that the girl had been trapped and that opening Door 14-C was necessary to release her.

When informed that no girl appeared in recorded footage, D-1904 accused research staff of altering the recording.

Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-03

Subject: D-5520

D-5520 was informed before exposure that viewing the area beyond Door 14-C would probably cause him to want the door opened.

The subject was shown the results of Tests {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01 and {{SCP_NUMBER}}-02 and instructed that, regardless of what he perceived, he was not to approach Door 14-C.

D-5520 observed the opening at 13:03.

At 13:04, he requested permission to leave.

Permission granted.

At 15:11, D-5520 asked a security officer whether maintenance personnel had inspected the mounting frame of Door 14-C.

At 19:32, D-5520 complained of chest pain and was transported to the Provisional Site medical unit.

At 19:46, he assaulted attending staff and attempted to return to the restricted access corridor leading to Chamber 14.

The subject was restrained.

A folded sheet of paper was discovered in D-5520's shoe. It contained a hand-drawn sketch of a narrow passage which D-5520 stated began behind Door 14-C.

No corresponding passage appeared on geological surveys then available to the Foundation.

Direct observational testing was suspended.

Door 14-C was welded shut on 03/16/2008.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-3: Door Configuration

During review of recovery documentation, Research Assistant H. Patel identified an unusual feature of Door 14-C.

The door opens toward Chamber 14.

The water-treatment employees had therefore been attempting to pull it open.

Door 14-C possesses no handle or opening mechanism on the chamber-facing surface. Damage recorded during recovery indicates that the employees inserted tools into the gap between the door and frame and attached a chain around its upper hinge assembly.

Before Door 14-C was permanently sealed, an optical probe was inserted through the existing gap without permitting an observer direct line of sight.

Images of the opposite face show a conventional lever handle.

No movement of this handle was recorded during the recovery operation or subsequent testing.

No exposed subject has explained why the individual they reported seeing beyond Door 14-C did not open the door from that side.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-4: Incident {{SCP_NUMBER}}-4

On 07/19/2011, Senior Researcher Morland requested permission to enter the restricted containment area and inspect Door 14-C.

The request was denied.

At 03:12 the following morning, Morland entered the containment access corridor using credentials belonging to Dr. ███████.

Security personnel intercepted him outside Door 14-A.

Morland surrendered without resistance.

A hacksaw, bolt cutters, hydraulic spreader, and two flashlights were recovered from a maintenance cart in his possession.

Morland had not directly observed the space beyond Door 14-C since Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01, more than three years earlier.

During interrogation, Morland denied intending to breach containment. He stated that he wished to inspect Door 14-C.

The following exchange occurred approximately two hours into the interview.

Agent Keller: You've spent three years studying this thing. You wrote half the containment procedures yourself.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: You know what happens to people who see through that door.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: And you still went down there.

Morland: Yes.

Keller: Why?

[Silence.]

Keller: What's behind the door?

Morland: We don't know.

Keller: What do you think is behind it?

Morland: Nothing.

Keller: Nothing?

Morland: The geological survey says solid limestone for another thirty-one meters.

Keller: Then what were you going to do with the spreader?

[Silence.]

Morland: Check.

Keller: Check what?

Morland: That it was still closed.

Keller: You can check that without opening it.

Morland: I know.

Keller: Then what did you need the tools for?

[Morland begins crying.]

Morland: I don't know.

Interview terminated.

Morland was removed from {{SCP_ID}} research.

Review of personnel records identified no comparable behavior among the nine individuals who had reviewed footage from Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-01 without direct exposure.

All personnel with a history of direct exposure were subsequently prohibited from participating in containment review.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-5: Structural Survey

On 11/██/2016, routine ultrasonic examination of the rock surrounding Door 14-C detected a discontinuity approximately 0.8 m beyond the threshold.

Previous geological surveys had indicated continuous limestone extending at least 31 m beyond the northern wall of Chamber 14.

Ground-penetrating radar was authorized from a Foundation excavation outside Chamber 14.

The first survey indicated an enclosed space measuring at least 9 m by 14 m. Its northern limit exceeded the effective range of the equipment.

A second survey conducted forty-eight hours later from the same location did not reproduce these dimensions.

Instead, the equipment indicated a narrow void approximately 2.3 m wide extending at least 46 m northward.

Calibration and independent testing of the equipment identified no malfunction.

The orientation and approximate course of the second void resembled the passage sketched by D-5520 during Test {{SCP_NUMBER}}-03.

A third survey was proposed and denied.

The following handwritten note was attached to the original report:

Don't measure it again.

First survey says there's a room.

Second says there's a hallway.

Original survey says there's thirty-one meters of solid limestone.

We have enough information to maintain containment.

— H.P.

The current physical extent of the space beyond Door 14-C is unknown.

Addendum {{SCP_NUMBER}}-6: Recovery Record Review

In 2022, original law-enforcement and Foundation records from the recovery of {{SCP_ID}} were digitized.

The ███████ County Sheriff's Department evidence manifest listed seventeen still photographs taken in Chamber 14.

The Foundation recovery report contained sixteen.

Photograph 17 was located in an unindexed forensic backup. It was taken at 05:41 on 03/11/2008, five minutes before Door 14-C was secured.

The image depicts Door 14-C open approximately 4 cm.

The camera flash illuminates several meters of rough stone surface beyond the threshold.

No human figure is visible.

The photograph was taken by Agent █████ ███████, one of the Foundation personnel involved in the final closure of Door 14-C. Records confirm that ███████ had reviewed employee identification photographs of all five missing water-treatment personnel before entering Chamber 14.

███████'s field notebook contains the following entry:

541.

Vale is inside.

About six meters back.

Photograph doesn't show him.

I checked again.

He's still there.

Close it now.

Door 14-C was secured at 05:46.

The official recovery report states that Thomas Vale was never located.

No attempt has been authorized to determine whether the figure observed by Agent ███████ was Thomas Vale.
